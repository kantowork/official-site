Installation & Setup

Get up and running in about a minute.

  1. Download and install the DMG
    Open KantoworkGestures-1.0.0.dmg and drag & drop Kantowork Gestures.app into your Applications folder.
  2. Launch the app
    Open Kantowork Gestures from Applications. A trackpad icon will appear in the top-right menu bar. (If macOS displays a security warning on first launch, follow the guide below to allow it.)
  3. Grant Accessibility permission
    To send keyboard shortcuts, follow the first-launch prompt and turn on Kantowork Gestures under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
  4. Register your gestures
    Click the menu bar icon and select "Open Settings... (Cmd + ,)" to assign shortcuts to global or per-app gestures.
If macOS blocks the app with a security warning on first launch:

Because this build is currently distributed with ad-hoc signing (not notarized), macOS may block launch with “Apple could not verify “Kantowork Gestures” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.” (or “cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified”). Since Right-click > Open is no longer available in macOS Sequoia (15) and later, allow the app using these steps:

  1. Click Done to close the warning dialog (do not click “Move to Trash”).
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the Security section, click Open Anyway next to “Kantowork Gestures was blocked to protect your Mac”, and authenticate with your password or Touch ID.
  4. When the confirmation dialog appears again, click Open Anyway to launch the app.

Note: On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or earlier, you can also right-click (Control + click) the app and choose “Open”. Advanced users or developers can alternatively remove the quarantine attribute in Terminal by running xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Kantowork Gestures.app".

Yes. Click "Deactivate This Mac" in the app's "🔑 License" tab, or remove the old Mac from the Polar Customer Portal, then reuse the same license key on your new Mac.
